# Data audit — September 10, 2026

Reviewed all 52 people in `src/data.js` with named news searches and follow-up checks of company announcements, personal profiles, and reporting. The baseline was the August 28, 2026 dataset; searches also covered earlier 2026 moves that might have been missed. This was a news and current-affiliation review, not a complete verification of every historical role.

Two missing intercompany moves were identified: Andrew Tulloch to Anthropic and Kyle Kosic to River AI. Demis Hassabis also has an unrecorded leadership transition within Alphabet. No additional substantiated employer changes were found for the other 49 people. Absence of a new report does not independently confirm continued employment.

- **Yuhuai Wu / Parade:** Crunchbase associates Wu with Parade, but this appears to conflate two people. Parade's CTO [Tony Wu](https://theorg.com/org/parade-ai/org-chart/tony-wu) has a Berkeley background, and [reporting places Parade's founding in 2015](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/mudflap-parade). This is insufficient evidence of a post-xAI move by Yuhuai Wu.
- **Andrej Karpathy:** Social-media speculation about an Anthropic departure did not yield substantiated departure reporting. Retained Anthropic; did not turn a changed-profile rumor into a career event.
- **Greg Yang:** Reports describe a step back into an informal advisory capacity. The existing endpoint for his operating role is retained; advisory ties are not treated as a new full-time employer.
- **Search limitations:** Some news and social-profile pages were available only through indexed text; live fetches were blocked or stale. In particular, Tulloch's personal site still listed Meta when opened. The reported qualifier and Kosic's bounded labels preserve these limitations instead of inventing exact dates.
